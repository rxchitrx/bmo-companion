## Design Context

### Users

This is a private macOS companion for Rachit. It owns a spare full-screen display and is primarily used through continuous natural voice conversation while Rachit works on the laptop screen. Controls should be immediately understandable, glanceable from a distance, and lightweight enough for a MacBook Air.

### Brand Personality

Playful, alive, and reassuring. The companion should feel like BMO from Adventure Time: expressive and friendly, but still dependable when it is listening, thinking, working, or waiting for permission.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the Adventure Time BMO artwork direction directly for this private build: mint body, friendly face, rounded screen, flat outlined shapes, and purposeful character animation. The companion takes over the display completely. Keep the body free of decorative console controls so the face remains dominant. Avoid floating desktop widgets, conventional assistant dashboards, glassmorphism, generic AI gradients, and dense technical chrome.

### Design Principles

1. Keep the animated face dominant; controls stay compact and secondary.
2. Make system state visible at a glance through expression, motion, and short plain-language labels.
3. Keep necessary controls quiet and compact while still meeting keyboard, focus, contrast, and reduced-motion needs.
4. Reveal work or configuration only when needed; the default experience is conversation, not a dashboard.
5. Prefer lightweight CSS and browser-native APIs over heavy visual dependencies.
